NAVSIM navtest: each model's 4-second plan, one scene at a time

The front camera at the moment of planning, and NAVSIM's bird's-eye view of the same moment. Press play: each plan's box moves as NAVSIM's simulated car drove that plan when it scored it, while the other road users replay what they did in the recording (NAVSIM v1 does not let them react).

This is the empty page template of navsim_eval/render_demo.py. Run the script with --html <file> to get a page with scenes.

Plans and their NAVSIM scores on this scene

Plan (tick to show) PDMS NC DAC TTC EP C

PDMS = NC × DAC × (5 TTC + 2 C + 5 EP) / 12. NC: no at-fault collision (0.5 when the object hit is static). DAC: every corner of the ego box stays on drivable area. TTC: time to collision stays above NAVSIM's bound. EP: progress along the route, relative to NAVSIM's own PDM-Closed planner. C: acceleration and jerk within comfort limits.

Key

        Map and road users in NAVSIM's own colours; each road user has a short line showing which way it faces. A box corner outside the grey road and green car-park areas zeroes DAC.

        What NAVSIM scores. One 4-second plan per scene: 8 poses, 0.5 s apart, drawn as the line and its markers. NAVSIM does not score those poses as they are: a simulated car (an LQR controller steering a kinematic bicycle model, in 0.1 s steps) follows the plan from the car's state at t = 0, and the collision, road and comfort checks run on that car. The moving boxes are that car, computed with NAVSIM's own simulator from the same metric cache the scores came from. So a box can drift off its line, by metres for a jerky plan or a sharp turn, and it is the box, not the line, that has to stay clear and on the road.

        Other road users. They are placed as NAVSIM places them when it scores: the logged boxes at 0.5 s steps, moved in straight lines in between. An object exists from the first to the last frame it was logged in (frames up to 5 s count), a missed frame inside a track is bridged, and an object logged in a single frame, even one after 4 s, stays at that spot for the whole plan.

        Keys. n / p: next or previous scene; space: play or pause; , / .: step half a second. Add #3 to the file's address to open scene 3, #3@2.5 to open it 2.5 s in, or #3@2.5,wide for the wider view.

        Data. This file embeds nuPlan camera images and map renders: keep it private and out of the repository.
