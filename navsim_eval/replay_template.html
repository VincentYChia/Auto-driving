Re-planning replay

Every 0.5 s along a 20 s stretch of a navtest drive, the model gets what NAVSIM gives a planner at that moment: the front camera, the last 1.5 s of the car's own poses, its speed, acceleration and the route command. From these it plans the next 4 s. The car then moves the way the human drove it (a replay, not closed loop), so you can watch each new plan react to what the camera shows. NAVSIM's score only uses the plan made at t = 0.
