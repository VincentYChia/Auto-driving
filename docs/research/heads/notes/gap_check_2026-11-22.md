# Literature check for gaps H1, H6 and H9 (2026-11-22)

Why: `docs/GAPS.md` moves an item to "Filled" only if a recorded literature search found no published version of the
same controlled comparison. Phase 4 completed the measurements for H1, H6 and H9 on 2026-11-22, and H9's entry asked
for its own check; H1 had none recorded beyond the head research (2026-11-19); H6 had one on 2026-11-21.

Scope: public papers and code only. Evidence marks: **V** read in the paper's text (through a page fetcher, which
returns quotes extracted by a model; the two decisive tables were re-read on their own, see the end), **R** abstract,
snippet or a page summary only, **D** our derivation.

## Results

### H1: a masked (discrete) diffusion head against continuous heads, one backbone, same data, NAVSIM

No published version of the same comparison (masked diffusion against DDIM, flow matching and anchored diffusion on
identical features and data) was found. The general question, discrete against continuous decoders on one backbone on
NAVSIM, is published:
- **DriveVLA-W0** (arXiv 2510.12796, v2 2025-12-18), Table 4, "Action experts reverse performance with data scaling":
  three action experts "initialized from an identical pretrained VLA model"; the VLA backbone is not frozen. NAVSIM
  (103k frames), PDMS: query-based (L1 regression) 88.4, flow matching 87.2, autoregressive FAST tokens 85.3. On 70M
  in-house frames the order reverses (autoregressive best by ADE and collisions). No masked-diffusion arm, no RL. **V**
- **ReCogDrive** (2506.08052): on the same VLM, text waypoints 84.1, a diffusion planner 86.5, 90.8 after DiffGRPO.
  The text output is autoregressive, not masked. **V**
- **AutoVLA-D** (2611.12382): on Qwen3-VL-4B, discrete action tokens 86.8 against numeric text waypoints 88.5, both
  decoded autoregressively; no diffusion or flow head. **V**
- Discrete-only NAVSIM papers, with no continuous arm on their own backbone: WAM-Diff (2512.11872), WAM-Flow
  (2512.06112), DriveFine (2602.14577), ReflectDrive (2509.20109), ReflectDrive-2 (2605.04647). **V**
- Not NAVSIM: Alpamayo-R1 (2511.00088) Table 12, autoregressive tokens against flow matching at the same size and data
  (minADE 0.681 against 0.644; internal data) **V**; Discrete Diffusion VLA (2508.20072) Table 13, same Qwen2.5-VL,
  LIBERO: discrete diffusion 95.1, continuous diffusion 94.9, FAST 91.1, autoregressive 81.2 **V**; arXiv 2610.20429,
  same PaliGemma-3B, RoboCasa: discrete flow 53.9% against continuous flow 52.7% **V**.

### H6: DDIM against flow matching (rectified flow) on one architecture, NAVSIM

Published in its general form:
- **DriftingPlanner** (arXiv 2611.01283, Bosch Research, submitted 2026-10-31), Table 5 "Drifting against iterative
  decoders": DDIM, DiffusionDrive-style anchor diffusion, flow matching and the paper's drifting decoder on a shared
  DINOv2 ViT-S/14 backbone, NAVSIM navtest, supervised only. PDMS at 1 / 5 / 10 / 20 function evaluations: DDIM 87.61 /
  89.02 / 89.65 / 89.83; anchor diffusion 90.06 / 89.36 / 89.41 / 89.41; flow matching 83.66 / 90.10 / 90.32 / 90.15;
  drifting 90.19 at 1. **R**: the title, authors, date and abstract were read verbatim, and the abstract's "within
  0.13 PDMS of the best diffusion or flow matching setting, which needs ten inference steps" matches these numbers
  (90.32 - 90.19); the table itself came back as a model-extracted summary, and whether its backbone is trained end to
  end or frozen could not be confirmed. Check the PDF before quoting the table. No discrete arm, no RL.
- Not the same comparison: DiffusionDrive (2411.15139; DDIM at 20 steps 84.6, truncated diffusion at 2 steps 85.7,
  no flow arm) **V**; GoalFlow (2503.05689; flow only) **V**; HDP (2602.22801; x0 / v / epsilon targets, open loop) **V**;
  Flow Planner (2510.11083, nuPlan; different architectures and training lengths) **V**; MeanFuser (2602.20060; MeanFlow
  against regression only) **V**; GenPlanner (2602.18812; mazes, checked 2026-11-21).

### H9: one RL recipe on masked and continuous heads, same backbone and reward

No published version found. Every NAVSIM paper with RL that was read applies it to one head type:
- AUGUstus (2610.16985): varies the RL noise scheme (Flow-GRPO, DanceGRPO, JaT-GRPO) on one flow head; the RL method
  changes, the head does not. **V**
- RL on discrete heads only (PDMS before to after): WAM-Diff, GSPO 86.6 to 91.0; WAM-Flow, GRPO 86.7 to 90.3; DriveFine
  86.7 to 89.6 (GRPO) and 90.8 (its full RL); ReflectDrive-2 87.7 to 89.1. **V**
- RL on continuous heads only: ReCogDrive 86.5 to 90.8; DiffAdapterVLA (2609.15322), DiffGRPO on DDPM adapters, 88.3 to
  90.3; FeasibleFlow (2609.23488), ReinFlow-style RL on MeanFlow, 88.9 to 90.1; DiffusionDriveV2 (2512.07745), anchored
  diffusion only, 91.2 with no matched row before RL. **V**. EponaV2 (2605.14696) and DriveWLA (2610.17903): RL on one
  flow head each. **R**
- The lifts look similar across families (about +1.2 to +4.4), but backbones, recipes and starting points all differ,
  so this says nothing either way. **D**
- Robotics: RLinf-VLA (2510.06710) runs the same PPO and GRPO on OpenVLA and OpenVLA-OFT (neither a diffusion head) **V**;
  dVLA-RL (2606.23623) applies PPO to a discrete-diffusion VLA only **V**; piRL (2510.25889) covers flow VLAs only **R**.
